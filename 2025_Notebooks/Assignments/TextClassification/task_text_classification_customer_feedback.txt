Problem Statement:
You work for a company that receives a lot of customer feedback through emails, chat, and social media. The company wants to automatically classify incoming feedback into predefined categories to speed up triaging and response.

Objective:
Classify customer feedback into one of the following categories:

Complaint
Praise
Query
Suggestion
Other



Suggested Workflow:
Data Cleaning: Lowercase, remove stopwords, lemmatize
EDA: WordCloud, class distribution, most common phrases
Vectorization: TF-IDF or Word Embeddings
Modeling: Train baseline and advanced models
Evaluation: Confusion Matrix, F1-Score, Accuracy
Deployment: Simple Streamlit app for real-time classification
